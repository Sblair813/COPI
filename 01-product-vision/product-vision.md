# COPI — Product Vision

## What is COPI?

**COPI — Core Product Integrity** is a product concept for managing product information and the work required to keep that information complete, accurate, and usable.

The initial module is **COPI Onboarding**, focused on bringing a new product from initial intake to a defined state of completeness.

The broader vision is for COPI to support additional product-information processes over time.

## The Problem

Product information often arrives incomplete, inconsistent, or in different formats.

The work of resolving those issues can require repeated manual effort:

* Finding what is missing
* Determining who needs to provide it
* Contacting vendors or other owners
* Reviewing responses
* Updating product information
* Checking the information again
* Following up when issues remain

COPI is intended to turn that work into a more structured and traceable process.

## Product Vision

> **Make product information integrity an ongoing, manageable process rather than a series of manual follow-ups.**

COPI should help teams understand:

* What information is required
* What is currently missing or invalid
* Who is responsible for resolving it
* What has already been requested or received
* Whether the latest information satisfies the requirement
* What still needs to happen
