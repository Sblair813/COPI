# COPI — Product Vision

## Product 

**COPI — Core Product Integrity**

COPI is a product integrity platform designed to support the management of product information throughout its lifecycle.

The platform is designed as a collection of configurable modules. Each module addresses a specific product-management challenge while sharing a common foundation for product data, rules, workflow, communication, and auditability.

## Initial Module

### COPI Onboarding

The first COPI module focuses on new product onboarding.

COPI Onboarding automates the process of identifying missing, incomplete, or invalid information in new product records and coordinating resolution with vendors or other responsible parties.

The onboarding process continues until the product record satisfies its defined requirements and is ready for downstream use.

## Vision

Create a consistent and traceable product-management framework that reduces manual effort, improves product data integrity, and provides visibility into the status and history of product information throughout its lifecycle.

## Product Principles

### 1. Industry Agnostic

COPI's core capabilities should not depend on a specific industry.

Industry-specific requirements should be configurable rather than embedded in the core platform.

### 2. Data Integrity Over Data Entry

COPI is not simply a mechanism for populating fields.

The platform should help determine whether product information is complete, valid, consistent, and appropriately supported.

### 3. Automation With Accountability

Automation should reduce repetitive work without eliminating visibility or control.

Actions, communications, responses, decisions, and status changes should be traceable.

### 4. Continuous Validation

Product integrity is not necessarily achieved once.

Product information may change over time, creating the opportunity for COPI to validate and manage product information beyond initial onboarding.

### 5. Modular Architecture

COPI should support additional capabilities without requiring the core product concept to be redesigned.

The initial onboarding capability establishes the foundation for future modules.

## Initial Product Outcome

For the initial COPI Onboarding module, success means:

> A new product can enter the organization with incomplete information, and the onboarding process can systematically identify what is missing, coordinate resolution, track what has occurred, and determine when the product record is ready for use.

## Future Opportunity

The initial onboarding capability establishes a foundation for additional product integrity capabilities.

Potential future modules may address areas such as:

* Ongoing product maintenance
* Product change management
* Product data validation
* Vendor data management
* Documentation management
* Product lifecycle monitoring
* Product integrity reporting

These capabilities are intentionally outside the initial onboarding scope and represent potential future product evolution.
